Staff Accountant
A growing company seeks an accountant to keep our books accurate and our reporting on time.
Responsibilities
Prepare journal entries, reconcile bank and general ledger accounts, process accounts payable and receivable, and support month-end close. Prepare financial statements, assist with budgeting and audits, and ensure tax compliance.
Requirements
Bachelor's degree in accounting or finance, hands-on experience with QuickBooks or similar ERP software, advanced Excel skills, attention to detail. CPA or progress toward CPA is a plus.
