Sales Representative
We need a motivated sales professional to grow revenue in an assigned territory.
Responsibilities
Generate leads, prospect new clients, deliver product presentations and negotiate contracts. Manage the sales pipeline in a CRM, meet or exceed quarterly quotas, and maintain strong customer relationships to drive repeat business and upselling.
Requirements
Proven track record in B2B or retail sales, excellent negotiation and closing skills, comfort with CRM tools, willingness to travel. Bachelor's degree preferred.
