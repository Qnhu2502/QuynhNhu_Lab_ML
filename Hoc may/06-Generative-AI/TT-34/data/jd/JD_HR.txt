Human Resources Generalist
Our people operations team is hiring an HR generalist to support employees and managers across the company.
Responsibilities
Run the full recruiting cycle: sourcing, screening, interviewing and onboarding new hires. Administer payroll and benefits, maintain employee records, handle employee relations issues and support performance review cycles. Ensure compliance with labor regulations and company policy.
Requirements
Two or more years of HR experience, knowledge of HRIS systems, strong communication and conflict resolution skills. Bachelor's degree in human resources, business or a related field. SHRM or PHR certification preferred.
